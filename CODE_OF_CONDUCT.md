# Code of conduct

Bongo is for everyone: beginners and experts, from every country and background.

**Please:**
- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new;
- give helpful, specific feedback about code, never about the person;
- accept that others may choose differently, and explain your reasons calmly.

**Not accepted:** insults, harassment, discrimination, sexual content, threats, or publishing someone's private information.

**Reporting:** if someone breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability"; mention that it's a conduct report), or contact a maintainer directly. Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributors that break these rules.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any Bongo community space.
